# Sources and dependency map

The primary result is the analytic argument in [the manuscript](paper/ptolemaic.tex). External sources below supply the conjecture, small endpoints, background or attribution. The source comparison is bounded; the [citation audit](CITATION_AUDIT.md) states exactly what the historical and current checks establish.

## Conjecture, endpoints and relevant predecessor

**Baker, M., Huh, J., Kummer, M., and Lorscheid, O. (2026).** *Lorentzian polynomials and matroids over triangular hyperfields 2: Analytic aspects*, version 2. [Versioned arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15375v2), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15375v2).

Conjecture 6.4 is the stated parity formula. Proposition 7.5 identifies `q(n)=P(n-1)`. Theorem 7.16 and Corollary 7.17 provide the four-point endpoint. Their earlier AI/Lean disclosure is not a formalization or endorsement of the present manuscript. The current comparison found v2, dated 18 August 2026, still the latest listed version.

**Ercan, E. (2026).** *Ptolemaic negative type and the values q(6) and q(7)*, version 1. [Versioned arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20606v1), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20606v1).

Theorem 1.1 gives `P(5)=log_2(9/4)` and `P(6)=1`. Its `P(5)` result is imported for the all-cardinality corollary; the manuscript independently proves all `m>=6`. Thus the first new cardinality relative to this predecessor is seven. Its partial-correlation and inversion framework requires attribution. Its external Bernstein certificate was not replayed in this package. The current comparison found only v1 listed, dated 20 August 2026.

**Korea Superintelligence Labs (2026).** *Sharp constants for Ptolemaic negative type: the p-anchor ladder, an impossibility window above q=1, and Bernstein cube inequalities on both sides of q=1*, version 3. [Publisher page](https://ideosphere.ai/papers/ptolemaic-negtype), [versioned PDF](https://ideosphere.ai/papers/ptolemaic-negtype-v3.pdf).

This materially relevant attribution was identified during the 26 September publication-intake refresh and was absent from the supplied manuscript bibliography. It has now been read back in the working public source's Section 1.1, bibliography and `references.bib`; the immutable input remains unchanged. The inspected locators are Section 11.1, equation (9), Theorems 11.1–11.3 (pp. 15–17); Section 12, equation (12) and Corollary 12.2 (pp. 17–18); and Section 15 (p. 26). These cover general anchor constants, complete-split thresholds/sharpness and an obstruction above exponent one, while leaving the general low-exponent estimate unresolved. The overlap concerns constants and witness thresholds, not a preceding all-cardinality lower-bound proof. Final rendering and frozen-archive checks are separate from this source readback.

## Classical background

**Schoenberg, I. J. (1938).** Metric spaces and positive definite functions. *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*, 44(3), 522–536. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1938-1501980-0). Historical negative-type/embedding correspondence. The finite Gram identity and embedding used here are written out in the manuscript; they are not claimed as new.

**Li, H., and Weston, A. (2010).** Strict p-negative type of a metric space. *Positivity*, 14, 529–545. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11117-009-0035-2), [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0695). Strict descent supports the optional strictness statement below the imported small endpoints, not the new cardinality induction. The supplied review explicitly reports an abstract-level comparison; it must not be relabeled full-text proof review.

**Sánchez, S. (2012).** On the supremal p-negative type of finite metric spaces. *Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications*, 389(1), 98–107. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2011.11.041), [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.0451). Inverse/determinant characterizations, including preprint Corollary 2.4, place the work in classical distance-matrix theory. The root-dependent mixed pairing `mu` in this manuscript is not identified with the classical metric M-constant.

**Fiedler, M. (2011).** *Matrices and graphs in geometry*. Cambridge University Press. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511973611). Background for augmented distance matrices and circumcenter identities. The supplied ledger reports metadata/front-matter inspection, not an audit of the entire book. No numbered theorem is imported; the needed identities are derived locally.

**Haynsworth, E. V. (1968).** Determination of the inertia of a partitioned Hermitian matrix. *Linear Algebra and its Applications*, 1(1), 73–81. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(68)90050-5). Attribution for inertia additivity; the exact block congruence used in the manuscript is displayed locally. Bibliographic verification is not full-text proof review.

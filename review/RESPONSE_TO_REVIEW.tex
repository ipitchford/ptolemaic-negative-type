\textbf{Sharp negative type of finite Ptolemaic metrics · version 1.0}\\
\textbf{Prepared for Evidence Press · 25 September 2026}

\subsection{Disposition}\label{disposition}

All required and recommended bounded revisions have been implemented.
The theorem and proof have not changed. The received review is preserved
unchanged in \texttt{RECEIVED\_REVIEW.md}; this response records
actions, not an additional specialist endorsement. The public manuscript
remains unrefereed and anonymously presented.

\subsection{Principal requests}\label{principal-requests}

\subsubsection{R4.1 --- Make the induction dependency
visible}\label{r4.1-make-the-induction-dependency-visible}

Immediately after Theorem 5.1 and before its proof, the manuscript now
states:

\begin{quote}
In the proof for m points, this theorem is applied with n+1=a+2≤m−1, so
its strictness hypothesis comes entirely from the outer induction.
\end{quote}

The definition of universal strictness and Appendix B are retained. The
inserted sentence changes exposition, not the hypothesis or argument.

\subsubsection{R4.2 --- Correct the current machine-readable numerical
summary}\label{r4.2-correct-the-current-machine-readable-numerical-summary}

\texttt{evidence/current/CURRENT\_NUMERICAL\_SUMMARY.json} is the
current reporting entry point. The four historical empty experiments now
have null minima and accepted count zero. Their initial sentinels remain
only in the preserved source files. The two components of the historical
mixed statistic cannot be reconstructed from its aggregate: they are
null with status \texttt{not\_separately\_recorded}, rather than
silently inferred.

The supplied follow-up experiment has been rerun with its original seed
and sampling design. It now records four distinct measurements:
centred-Gram minimum eigenvalue, minimum on an orthonormal zero-sum
subspace, the split-vector gap, and its Rayleigh quotient. Each has an
accepted-sample count. All 2,400 arrays and measurements are saved at
full floating-point precision and independently recomputed by the
reporting verifier.

The adversarial rerun retains all 160 attempts, including solver status,
convergence flag, iterations, constraint slacks and
exceptions/rejections. All 160 met the feasibility tolerance; 150 also
reported convergence. Ten tolerance-feasible runs did not converge
according to the solver. Their inclusion is explicit and they are
excluded from the converged-only maxima. The maximum recorded deficit,
about 3.25×10⁻¹², is not a validated counterexample under a 10⁻⁸
feasibility allowance.

The reporting verifier checks every stored array, every aggregate and
three deliberate null/count failures. Original reports and scripts
remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

\subsubsection{R4.3 --- Identify the advance over the
predecessor}\label{r4.3-identify-the-advance-over-the-predecessor}

Section 1.1 now says explicitly that the new numerical values begin at
P(7); P(6) is independently recovered. It explains how star packing
handles nonnegative inverse vectors in arbitrary dimension and how
recursive inverse-entry control handles the other signs, avoiding
separate higher-cardinality case analyses. Attribution for inversion,
Schur complements, the conjecture, smaller endpoints and complete split
witnesses is retained.

\subsection{Verification and scope}\label{verification-and-scope}

The original 65-file archive passed full replay. Core exact, symbolic
and numerical reports and the 13-group manuscript checks reproduced
identically; two exploratory reports differed in floating-point values.
The new revision verifier passes 16 checks, including exact seven-point
and eight-point calculations. All current numerical measurements have
been recomputed from their stored arrays, with explicit numerical
tolerances. The immutable publication replay records exact and numerical
comparisons separately.

The revised PDF has 15 pages. Theorem and equation labels remain
unchanged. The proof for m≥6 still does not import the external
five-point certificate. A pointwise endpoint argument still avoids
inversion of singular endpoint matrices. The prepared manuscript does
not imply website deployment, DOI registration, newly assigned
licensing, or authenticated external peer review.
